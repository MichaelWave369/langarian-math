# Governance Note — Three Gates

This v0.3.1 clarification was prompted by external review emphasizing a durable distinction:

```text
Syntax / Integrity Gate
        ↓
Formal Eligibility Gate
        ↓
Reality Gate
```

The review observed that the workbench's strongest contribution is research governance: provenance, receipts, bounded claims, explicit non-claims, versioning, and separation of computation from interpretation.

The public rename from **Proof Gate** to **Formal Eligibility Gate** follows the reviewer's precision concern: the gate determines whether an object may enter formal proof review; it does not declare that proof has succeeded.

The proposed Reality Gate remains future work and must preserve the independence of formal validity from empirical validity.

The governing principle adopted by this release is:

> **The ledger serves reality, not the author.**

This note records influence and rationale. It does not grant authority to a person, model, or theory. The architecture stands or falls by its definitions, implementation, tests, evidence, and future correction.
